Our Story

More Than
a Haircut

Sunset All Stars is a community barbershop in the heart of Sunset Park, Brooklyn. We're not just about cuts — we're about the experience: the music, the conversations, and the care that goes into every single service.

Led by Luis and a team of skilled barbers, we serve everyone from young kids to working professionals. Our bilingual (English & Spanish) team makes every client feel right at home.
