Our Story

MORE THAN
JUST A CUT

Sunset All Stars isn't just a barbershop — it's a Brooklyn institution. Nestled in the heart of Sunset Park at 369 50th St, we've built a community around the art of grooming, the rhythm of salsa and bachata, and the shared pride of looking your absolute best.

Led by Luis and a team of highly skilled barbers, every visit is a personalized experience. We speak your language — literally. Our bilingual (English/Spanish) team welcomes everyone from young kids to working professionals, from first visits to lifelong loyalists.
